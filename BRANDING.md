# Naming and branding

The MIT license permits modification, commercial use and redistribution of public
Cast code. It does not grant trademark rights or imply endorsement. Cast and Cast
Pro identify the owner's products; no trademark registration is asserted here.

Forks should choose a distinct product name, application/package IDs, icons and
update channel where users could confuse the fork with an official release. Preserve
accurate copyright/license attribution. Descriptive references to upstream Cast are
welcome. This naming policy adds no restriction to MIT copyright permissions; final
trademark policy and ownership remain subject to owner/legal review.
