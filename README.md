cast
===

`cast` is a minimal Linux tool that combines your screen and webcam into a virtual camera, with smooth zoom, presentation overlays, and audio recording—all controlled from your keyboard.


### Your screen. Your camera. Your call.

`cast` turns your Linux desktop and webcam into one presentation feed for video calls, demos, tutorials, and recordings.
Share an entire monitor, a window, or a selected region, with configurable camera layouts, shapes, and positioning.

Guide attention with smooth cursor zoom, click highlights, and optional keystroke overlays.
Switch layouts and sources through simple CLI commands bound to your preferred shortcut manager.
Pause live output for privacy, pause recording without leaving a gap, or record your presentation with microphone and selected desktop audio.

Written in C with a focus on small code and minimal dependencies, cast uses a runtime configuration file in your XDG config directory.
It puts presentation controls at your fingertips, without a scene editor or heavyweight interface.
